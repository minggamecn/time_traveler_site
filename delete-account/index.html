Account & data

Delete your data

How to delete your data for Time Traveler: Love & Choices, by Ming Zhu.

There is no account to delete

The Game does not have accounts or sign-in, and it does not send your progress or personal information to us. Everything it saves is on your own device, so you can delete it yourself at any time, as shown below.

On this page

  1. Delete your saved progress
  2. Delete device backups
  3. Purchase records
  4. Ask us to delete data we hold

1. Delete your saved progress

On your Android device, do one of the following:

  • Clear the Game’s data: open Settings → Apps → Time Traveler → Storage & cache → Clear storage. (Menu names vary slightly by device.)
  • Uninstall the Game: press and hold the Time Traveler icon, then choose Uninstall.

Either option permanently deletes your character name, story progress, choices, collection and settings from that device. This cannot be undone, because we have no copy.

2. Delete device backups

If Android backup is on, your device backup in your Google account may include a copy of your progress. You can manage or delete device backups in the Google One app, or at one.google.com under Storage → Device backup. These backups are controlled by you and Google; we cannot access or delete them.

3. Purchase records

Records of what you bought are kept by Google Play in your Google account, under Google’s policies, and let you restore your purchases later. Deleting the Game’s data does not delete these records or cancel your purchase. To manage your Google Play history, see your Google Account data & privacy settings.

4. Ask us to delete data we hold

The only personal information we may hold is email you have sent us. To have it deleted, or to ask any question about your data, email us:

wdswmz@gmail.com

Please send the request from the address you used to contact us, so we know it is yours. We will delete the messages and confirm within 30 days. We will never ask for your password.

If a future version of the Game adds optional accounts or cloud saves, it will also add a way to delete your account inside the Game, and this page will be updated with the steps.
