Support

Answers to common questions about Time Traveler: Love & Choices. If you need anything else, we’re happy to help.

Contact us

wdswmz@gmail.com

Tell us your device model, Android version, and what happened. For a purchase problem, include the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) from your receipt email. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I unlock the rest of the story?

Chapters 1–3 are free. Tap “Unlock the full story” to buy Volume 1, which unlocks chapters 4–24 with a single purchase through Google Play. Future chapters will arrive as Volume 2.

I bought Volume 1, but the chapters are still locked.

Make sure you are connected to the internet and signed in to Google Play with the account you used to buy it, then open Settings in the Game and tap “Restore purchases”. If a payment is still pending, the chapters unlock once Google Play confirms it. If it still doesn’t work, email us with your order number.

I got a new phone. Can I keep my purchase and progress?

Your purchase: yes. Install the Game on the new phone with the same Google account and tap “Restore purchases” in Settings.

Your progress is saved on the device, not online. If you set up the new phone from an Android backup of the old one, your progress may come with it. Otherwise the story starts again from the beginning, with everything you bought still unlocked.

My progress disappeared.

Progress is lost if the Game’s storage is cleared or the Game is uninstalled. We don’t keep a copy, so we can’t restore it for you. If the Game finds a damaged save, it automatically tries its last good backup first. Purchases are never lost; use “Restore purchases” to get them back.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are refunded through Google Play. Within 48 hours of buying, you can usually request a refund at play.google.com → Order history. After that, see Google Play’s refund help, or email us and we will look into it.

How do I change the language?

The Game starts in your device language if it is supported: English, 简体中文, Español or Bahasa Indonesia. Otherwise it starts in English. To switch, open Settings in the Game and choose Language.

Can I change my character’s name?

Yes. Open Settings in the Game and edit “Your name in the story”.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed, and the Game plays offline. You only need a connection to buy or restore purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Everything the Game saves is on your device. See Delete your data for the steps.
