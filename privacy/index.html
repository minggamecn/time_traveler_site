Privacy policy

This policy explains how the game Time Traveler: Love & Choices (the “Game”, Android package com.mz.ttlc) and this website handle your information.

Effective: 2026-10-24 · Last updated: 2026-10-26

In short

  • The Game has no account and no sign-in. It shows no ads and contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. We do not receive it, and we cannot see it.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never receive your payment details.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information.

1. Who we are

The Game and this website are made and operated by Ming Zhu, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the handling of information described in this policy.

Questions or requests about privacy: wdswmz@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue your journey, the Game saves the following in its private storage on your device:

  • the name you choose for your character in the story;
  • your story progress, choices, companions, Memories, items and Expeditions;
  • which volumes you own, and the Google Play order numbers of your purchases (so that a purchase is counted once, and a refunded one is removed);
  • settings such as language, music and reduced motion.

This information stays on your device. The Game does not send it to us or to anyone else, and we have no copy of it. Other apps cannot read it. If you clear the Game’s storage or uninstall it, this information is deleted.

3. Purchases through Google Play

Chapters 1–3 are free. If you buy Volume 1 or any later content, the purchase is made through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and your Google account details under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play’s terms. We never receive your card number, billing address or other payment details.

To unlock what you bought, the Game asks Google Play on your device which items your Google account owns, and receives the product, order number and purchase status. This is also how “Restore purchases” works on a new device. These details are kept only on your device, as described above. We can see standard order reports in the Google Play Console (for example order number, product, price, country and refund status), which Google provides to all developers for accounting, tax and refund handling.

4. Android device backup

If backup is turned on for your Android device, Android may include the Game’s saved progress in your device backup, so that it can be restored when you set up a new phone. These backups are created and stored by Google in your Google account under Google’s terms. We cannot access them. You control backup in your device settings (usually Settings → Google → Backup).

5. Permissions and network use

The Game asks for internet, network-state and Google Play billing access only so it can reach Google Play for purchases. It uses a secure (HTTPS) connection. It does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or location.

Google Play’s billing library, which the Game uses to make purchases, may send Google technical information about how it is working, such as whether a billing request succeeded. Google handles this under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive it.

The Game plays fully offline once installed. You need a connection only to buy or restore purchases.

6. What we do not collect

The current version of the Game does not collect or send us:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details;
  • advertising IDs, device IDs or your location;
  • analytics, usage statistics or crash reports;
  • your progress, choices or anything you type into the Game.

The Game contains no advertising and no third-party tracking.

7. This website

This website is a set of plain pages hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies of its own, uses no analytics or advertising, and has no forms. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep them secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, such as your device model or a description of a problem. We use it only to answer you and to help with your request. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail), which processes messages under its own privacy policy.

9. Keeping and deleting information

  • Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. See Delete your data for step-by-step instructions.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google for as long as Google requires. Your right to the content you bought is tied to your Google account, so deleting the Game’s data does not cancel a purchase; you can restore it later.
  • Emails are kept for as long as we need them to handle your request, and for no more than two years afterward. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because the Game keeps your information only on your device, you can view and delete it yourself at any time. For anything we hold, such as emails you sent us, write to wdswmz@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

11. Children

The Game is a romance story intended for teens and adults, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the Game changes how it handles information, for example if a future version adds optional online features such as cloud saves. The “Last updated” date above shows when it last changed. If a change affects how your information is used, we will tell you in the Game or on its Google Play listing before the change takes effect, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
